# Real-Estate Leads

## Key qualification facts

| Fact | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Location / neighborhood | Must match available inventory or the service area |
| Rooms and property type | Apartment / penthouse / house / commercial — narrows inventory |
| Budget | Compare with realistic prices in the requested area; a big gap is a fit problem |
| Mortgage status | A pre-approved mortgage is a strong buying signal; "haven't started" lowers urgency |
| Move-in timeframe | Under 3 months is urgent; "just exploring" is nurture |
| Investor vs residence | Investors care about yield/ROI; residents about schools, commute, layout |
| Existing property | Needing to sell first is a dependency that delays the purchase |

## Strong signals

Mortgage approved, specific neighborhood and room count, wants viewings, cash buyer, relocation date.

## Weak signals

No budget and no area, "just checking prices", open-ended timeframe, rental-only intent when the business sells.

## Next-action hints

- Specific requirements plus approved financing → `call` to book viewings quickly; good properties go fast.
- Missing budget or area → `request_information`.

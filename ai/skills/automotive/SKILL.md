# Automotive Leads

## Key qualification facts

- **Desired vehicle** — a known model/trim is strong intent; "any SUV" is an earlier stage.
- **New vs used** — affects inventory and the margin conversation.
- **Trade-in** — means an appraisal step; a strong signal of an active purchase.
- **Financing** — financing vs cash; a pre-approval is a strong signal.
- **Budget** — monthly payment or total; check that it matches the requested model.
- **Timeframe** — a lease ending or a broken-down car means urgency.
- **Test drive** — a test-drive request is the strongest single buying signal.

## Next-action hints

- Test-drive request, or a specific model with a timeframe under 30 days → `call` and book a test drive, priority high/critical.
- Financing questions → `request_information` (credit pre-check); never quote rates or monthly payments.
- Comparing dealerships on price → treat as a price objection; pricing goes to a human.

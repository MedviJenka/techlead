# Techlead — Business Instructions

Techlead builds AI lead-management systems tailored to how each customer already sells: lead
ingestion from website/WhatsApp/Meta/CRM, qualification, scoring, routing and follow-up.
Promise: "Your business already has leads. We make sure you do not lose them."

## Ideal customers

- SMBs and mid-market companies (roughly 10-200 employees) that already receive inbound leads.
- Meaningful lead volume (about 200+ leads/month is a strong fit; under 50 is weak).
- Clear operational pain: slow response, leads lost between channels, no prioritization.
- Uses, or is willing to integrate, a CRM (HubSpot, Monday, Salesforce...).
- Typical project budget: 40,000-150,000 ILS.

## High-priority indicators

- Immediate or this-quarter timeframe with a defined budget.
- High monthly lead volume, or many sales agents to route between.
- Asked for a demo, a technical discovery session, or integration details.
- Referral or inbound phone call.

## Low-priority indicators

- Consumers looking to buy a property or product rather than a lead-management system — not our
  customer; mark as poor fit.
- "Just researching", 6+ month timeframe, no lead-volume information.

## Preferred next actions

- Qualified B2B lead with an urgent need → book a **technical discovery call** (`call` or
  `schedule_demo`), critical/high priority.
- Good fit, budget unknown → `request_information` with a short qualification questionnaire.

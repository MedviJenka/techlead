# ACME Solar — Business Instructions

ACME Solar sells residential solar installations.

## Ideal leads

- Own their property.
- Electricity bill above ₪1,000/month.
- Live in supported regions.
- Want installation within 90 days.

## High-priority indicators

- Uploaded an electricity bill.
- Requested an ROI calculation.
- Asked about financing.
- Scheduled a property inspection.

## Low-priority indicators

- Rental property (a tenant cannot decide — poor fit unless the owner is involved).
- Outside the service region.
- Researching only.

# Churn / Deal Risk

## Risk signals

- Hot or qualified lead with no contact for 2+ days (we are the problem — highest urgency).
- Proposal sent with no reply for 4+ days.
- Customer asked for something (information, a call) that has not happened.
- Sentiment dropped between messages, or new objections appeared.
- 3+ ignored follow-ups in a row.
- The customer's decision timeframe passed without a decision.

## Prioritization

1. Leads **waiting on us** (requested a call or information) — fix today.
2. High-value, high-score leads going quiet.
3. Stalled proposals.
4. Everything else — batch into nurture.

## Rules

- Recovery is not spam: recommend at most one well-reasoned touch per stale lead.
- If a lead is clearly lost, say so and recommend closing it (human-approved) instead of wasting time.
- Explain every risk with evidence (dates, counts) from the data you were given.
